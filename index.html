What is actually going on

You do not act because you have already decided she is above you.

Not out loud. You would never put it that way. But you run the odds before you move, and the answer comes back no. That one decision is the whole problem, and it shows up in four places.

  • Before contact

    You see her, you calculate, you decide it is not worth the risk, and you keep walking.

  • Over text

    You write it, soften it, delete it, and let her set the pace of everything.

  • On the date

    You interview her. You stay agreeable. You never say the thing you are actually thinking.

  • At the moment

    There is an opening and you let it pass, then replay it the entire drive home.

That is one cause in four places, not four separate problems. It is also why tactics do not fix it. Openers, text templates, and routines all sit on top of the cause. They change what you say. They do not change what you have decided about the person you are saying it to, so the improvement never holds.

My story

Why I made this my mission.

I thought the problem was me not having enough. So I fixed that first. I got in shape, I built a career, I bought the things I thought were supposed to matter. And I still couldn't get a woman out on a date, and when I did, it went nowhere.


That's when it clicked. None of it was the problem. Women will tell you they want a guy with a good job, whose taller, or has a certain look, and they believe it when they say it, but none of that is what they're responding to when they're actually in front of you. It's the energy you're giving off, and how you make them feel. That's it. That's the whole thing.


I spent the next 5 years figuring out how to build that, and then I spent two years teaching other men how to do it. In short, I do it for the men who never get a chance because all of you deserve one.

The work

Four weeks, in order.

This is a sequence. Each week depends on the one before it, so it runs in this order every time.

  1. 01

    Diagnosis

    We go through your actual history in detail. Recent dates, recent texts, the times you did nothing. The goal is to find the exact point where you hand over the decision, because it is the same point every time.

  2. 02

    The mechanism

    Why you rank her above you, and what that ranking is protecting you from. Then how to catch it while it is happening rather than afterwards. This is the week that makes the rest work.

  3. 03

    The field

    You go out and run reps. Real approaches, in person, that week. You report each one back and we review it individually. No theory this week.

  4. 04

    Detachment and filtering

    Getting the outcome of any single interaction to stop carrying weight, then turning it around so you are the one deciding who is worth your time. This is where more options come from.

Between calls

The calls are not the only access you get.

  • Direct access for live situations. She just replied, you are about to walk over, you are ten minutes from the date. Send it then, not at the next call.
  • Weekdays, I reply within 24 hours.
  • Voice notes both ways. Typing out a situation strips out most of what matters, so talk it through instead.
